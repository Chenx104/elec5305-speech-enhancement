## Final Research Project

**When Better Denoising Does Not Mean Better Speaker Verification: An Empirical Study of Speech Enhancement and Speaker Identity Preservation**

The final project investigates the relationship between speech enhancement quality and speaker verification performance using MATLAB spectral subtraction, DeepFilterNet3, and ECAPA-TDNN.

The complete implementation, reproducibility instructions, verified experimental results, and figures are available in the [Final Project folder](final_project/).
